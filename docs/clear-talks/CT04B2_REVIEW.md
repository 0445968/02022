## Critical read-only preflight and design decision
Hosted editorial_profiles.account_id UNIQUE FK to profiles(id) ON DELETE SET NULL. There are 2 editorial profiles, both already linked; all 18 published stories have author_id and resolve their byline by matching account_id. Changing account_id risks historical attribution. No eligible unlinked author currently exists for a new staff account. **Do not alter public author/account links or existing stories.** Private association is not public linkage, no automatic creation/publication or account reassignment; actual future editorial attribution model must be reviewed under CT-11. Existing byline may be associated only when unlinked or already linked to exactly that membership's Auth account. New profiles must be separately created/reviewed in Authors workflow, not fabricated or automatically published here.
- Hosted service_role lacked both SELECT and UPDATE on editorial_profiles; postgres has grant option on five needed columns. Migration grants ONLY SELECT(id,account_id,byline_name,slug,byline_status) to service_role, no UPDATE or table-wide SELECT. This is an explicit new privilege requiring release review. Legacy account changes may occur concurrently outside staff lock; private directory detects conflict and allows unlink, and any future public integration must revalidate exact relationship. No claim of serializing legacy author edits.
- New FK ON DELETE RESTRICT preserves approved private association against deletion of its editorial profile; explicitly unlink first. Review interaction with legacy Authors UI before activation.
- No automatic public byline changes or permission grant from private approval. Existing published attribution and reader identity untouched.
